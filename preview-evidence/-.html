Starlink Tips & Setup Guide

Get the best performance from your Starlink

TIP 01

Clear Sky View

Mount the dish high with a clear view of the sky - no trees, rooftops or buildings blocking it. Even a small obstruction cuts speeds significantly.

TIP 02

Use the Starlink App

Download the free Starlink app before installation. It has an obstruction checker - point your phone at the sky to find the best mounting spot.

TIP 03

Power Stability

Use a surge protector or UPS. Starlink draws about 50-75W and needs stable power. Power surges can damage the router. An inverter/UPS keeps it running during load shedding.

TIP 04

WiFi Range

The Starlink router covers a standard home. For larger properties, add a mesh WiFi extender. Position the router centrally for best coverage throughout the building.

TIP 05

Rain & Weather

Starlink works in rain and most weather conditions. Heavy storms may briefly slow speeds. The dish is weatherproof and has a built-in heater for ice/frost (though rare in Zambia).

TIP 06

Subscription Management

Manage your plan directly through the Starlink app or website. You can pause service when you travel, change plans, or add extra data if needed. We can assist with account setup.

Tech Tips

Quick tips to keep your devices running longer

Glass Protector

A good glass protector is cheaper than replacing a screen. Fit one early and save yourself a bigger repair later.

Storage Space

Keep free storage available so your phone stays smooth, your camera works properly, and apps do not start crashing.

Heat Control

Too much heat damages batteries and performance. Do not leave devices charging in the sun, in hot cars, or under pillows.

Water Damage

If a phone gets wet, power it off immediately and bring it in fast. The quicker the action, the better the recovery chance.

Backups and Data

Back up before repair or software work. iCloud, Google, or local backups save you from the hardest kind of loss.

Battery Health

Try to keep charge habits balanced. Constant overheating and full overnight charging shorten battery life faster than most people realize.

BATTERY HEALTH

Keep your battery between 20-80% for longest lifespan. Avoid overnight charging. Check health: iPhone - Settings > Battery > Battery Health.

BACKUPS

Always back up before any repair or software work. Use iCloud or iTunes for iPhone. Google Photos for Android. We are not responsible for data loss.

TEMPERATURE

Keep phones away from direct sunlight and heat. High temperatures damage batteries permanently. Don't charge in hot cars.

WATER DAMAGE

If water gets in, turn off immediately, don't charge, and bring it in. Don't put it in rice. Fast action improves recovery chances.

SCREEN PROTECTOR

A glass protector is much cheaper than a screen replacement. Get one fitted - K100 including fitting at Shop 102.

STORAGE

Keep at least 15% storage free for smooth performance. Full storage causes slowdowns, app crashes and poor camera quality.
